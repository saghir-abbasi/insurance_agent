Q1: Can a applicant obtain life insurance if they were previously declined due to a memory-related condition?

Category: Underwriting/Eligibility

Answer: Yes. Even if a customer was previously declined by another insurer (such as Mutual of Omaha) due to taking memory-related medications, they may still qualify for whole life insurance through alternative carriers like Guarantee Trust Life (GTL) based on their specific health profile and medication details.

Confidence: High

Q2: Does Guarantee Trust Life (GTL) whole life insurance offer cash value benefits?

Category: Riders/Add-ons

Answer: Yes. Guarantee Trust Life whole life insurance includes a cash value benefit, allowing policyholders to accumulate additional money over time on top of the face amount, which can be accessed or borrowed against for emergencies or living needs according to the policy terms.

Confidence: High

Q3: How much does a $10,000 whole life insurance policy with Guarantee Trust Life cost for a qualified senior non-smoker?

Category: Premium/Pricing

Answer: For an eligible customer in their 70s who is a non-smoker, a $10,000 whole life insurance policy with Guarantee Trust Life costs $151.99 per month.

Confidence: High

Q4: How does whole life insurance differ from term life insurance policies that may expire or cancel?

Category: Policy Terms/Duration

Answer: Whole life insurance is designed to remain in force for the rest of the insured's life and never expires or terminates based on age or health changes, unlike certain term life insurance policies that may expire or cancel over time.

Confidence: High

Q5: What is the review period and approval process after submitting an application for final expense whole life insurance?

Category: Policy Terms/Duration

Answer: After submitting an electronic application for review, paperwork is delivered within 3 to 4 working days. The applicant then receives a 30-day review period to review the policy details or discuss them with family and friends.
