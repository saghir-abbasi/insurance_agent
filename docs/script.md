# Final Expense — Agent First-Call & LA Transfer Script

## Call Objective

The first agent handles the conversation through Section 6, qualifies the customer, explains the Final Expense product, and then transfers the qualified call to a licensed agent (LA). The first-agent script ends at the transfer.

---

## 1. Opening & Call Reason

**Agent:** Hi, am I speaking with [Customer Name]?

**Customer:** Yes.

**Agent:** Hi [Customer Name], my name is [Agent Name]. I’m a product specialist. The reason for my call today is to help you look at Final Expense life insurance options that may help cover funeral, burial, or other final expenses and potentially leave something behind for your loved ones. I just need to ask you a few questions to see what options may be available for you. Is that okay?

**Customer:** Yes.

**Agent:** Perfect. I have you currently in the state of [State], is that correct?

**Agent:** Great. And what’s your ZIP code?

---

## 2. Initial Explanation & Rapport

**Agent:** Thank you. Before we get into anything else, I just want to let you know who I am, what I do, and how this type of plan can benefit you and your family. Then you can tell me what you’d like to do next. Sounds fair?

**Agent:** We are an independent insurance brokerage company working with top-rated insurance companies, and we can shop available options to help find an affordable plan for you.

**Agent:** There are a few incentive-based questions I need to ask. First, are you a smoker or non-smoker?

**Agent:** Do you use a checking account or a savings account?

**Agent:** Have you been living in the same state for the past 12 months?

**Agent:** Based on your answers, I’ll check which available options may fit your situation. The policy and pricing can depend on your age, health conditions, and other factors.

---

## 3. Privacy & Basic Qualification

**Agent:** I’m going to ask you some basic health questions. Your personal and health-related information should be handled confidentially according to the applicable privacy requirements. You can answer the qualification questions Yes or No to the best of your knowledge.

**Agent:** First, please confirm your date of birth.

**Agent:** What is your height and weight?

---

## 4. Coverage Need / Customer Goal

**Agent:** A lot of people I speak with consider this type of policy for a few reasons: to cover burial expenses, cremation expenses, or to leave some additional money behind for their loved ones.

**Agent:** If you decide to look at a plan, what would be most important to you — covering burial or cremation expenses, or leaving something behind for your loved ones?

**Agent:** Is this going to be your first policy or an additional one?

---

## 5. Initial Health Qualification

**Agent:** Alright, I’ll help you with that. I’m going to ask a few basic health questions. Please answer Yes or No to the best of your knowledge.

**Agent:** Are you currently hospitalized, confined to any nursing facility, a bed, or a wheelchair?

**Agent:** Have you had any heart attack, stroke, cancer, or kidney dialysis before?

**Agent:** Do you use oxygen or a nebulizer, or have CHF, dementia, AIDS, or HIV?

**Agent:** How many medications do you take a day?

**Agent:** What purposes do you take those medications for?

---

## 6. Final Expense Product Explanation

**Agent:** Thank you for providing that information. The type of policy we are discussing is Final Expense whole life insurance. The supplied script describes it as coverage intended to remain in force according to the policy terms and provide a death benefit to the family.

**Agent:** People generally consider coverage for a few situations: they may have no coverage and don’t want a loved one to have to pay the bill; they may have some coverage but not enough because funeral costs have increased; or they may already have coverage and want to leave something additional behind for a loved one.

**Agent:** The supplied script also presents the benefits and premiums as remaining the same for the life of the policy, subject to the actual policy terms.

**Agent:** The supplied script states that no physical examination or blood testing is required and discusses cash value benefits. Cash value is described as value that may accumulate in addition to the face amount and may be accessible according to the policy terms.

---

## 7. LA Transfer Protocol — END OF FIRST-AGENT SCRIPT

### Transfer Trigger

Once the customer has completed the Section 6 qualification/product discussion and remains interested in exploring coverage, the Agent should move to the transfer.

**Agent:** “Based on the information you’ve provided, the next step is to have a licensed agent go over the available options with you and answer any carrier-specific questions. I’m going to connect you with a licensed agent now. Please stay on the line for me.”

### Transfer Protocol

- Keep the customer engaged while the transfer is being initiated.
- Do not disconnect before the LA connection is established.
- Provide the receiving LA with the required customer information/qualification data through the approved transfer system or warm-transfer process.
- The first Agent should not promise approval, guarantee a specific carrier, or represent that the customer has already been approved.
- Once the LA answers and the customer is successfully connected, the first Agent ends the call according to the approved transfer procedure.
